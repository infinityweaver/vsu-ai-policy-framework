\section{Glossary}\label{app:glossary}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=0.5cm,style=nextline]
  \item[AI agent] An AI system that can plan and take actions (e.g., use tools, edit files, send messages) toward a goal with some autonomy.
  \item[AI assistant] A conversational or embedded AI system that responds to user prompts.
  \item[AI-use level] A course- or assessment-level declaration (0--4) of how AI may be used.
  \item[AI-text detector] Software that estimates whether text was machine-generated; known to be unreliable.
  \item[Authentic assessment] A task resembling real-world work, often contextual and process-visible.
  \item[De-identification] Removing or masking information that identifies a person.
  \item[DPO] Data Protection Officer.
  \item[Generative AI] AI that produces new content such as text, images, code, audio, or video.
  \item[Personal data] Information that identifies or can reasonably identify an individual; see the Data Privacy Act.
  \item[Process evidence] Drafts, version history, notes, or reflections showing how work was produced.
  \item[Tier A/B/C] The three levels of tool-use review (open, listed, reviewed) in Section~\ref{sec:tools}.
\end{description}
