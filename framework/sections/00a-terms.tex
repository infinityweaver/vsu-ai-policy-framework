\section{Terms and Abbreviations}\label{sec:terms}

These terms and abbreviations are used throughout the framework.

\subsection{Abbreviations}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=0.5cm,style=nextline]
  \item[AI] Artificial intelligence.
  \item[BOR] Board of Regents of the University.
  \item[CHED] Commission on Higher Education.
  \item[CMO] CHED Memorandum Order; \cmo{} is the national framework on AI in higher education.
  \item[DPO] Data Protection Officer.
  \item[HEI] Higher education institution.
  \item[IT] Information Technology.
  \item[NPC] National Privacy Commission.
  \item[RA] Republic Act.
  \item[TWG] Technical Working Group for AI Policy and Governance, the policy owner of this framework.
  \item[UADCO] University Administrative Council.
  \item[UAC] University Academic Council.
  \item[\vsu{}] Visayas State University.
\end{description}

\subsection{Terms}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=0.5cm,style=nextline]
  \item[AI agent] An AI system that can plan and take actions (e.g., use tools, edit files, send messages) toward a goal with some autonomy.
  \item[AI assistant] A conversational or embedded AI system that responds to user prompts.
  \item[AI Use Declaration] A short statement accompanying a submission that states whether and how AI was used (Section~\ref{sec:integrity}, Appendix~\ref{app:declarations}).
  \item[AI-use level] A course- or assessment-level declaration (0--4) of how AI may be used.
  \item[AI-text detector] Software that estimates whether text was machine-generated; its output is never sufficient on its own as evidence of misconduct.
  \item[Authentic assessment] A task resembling real-world work, often contextual and process-visible.
  \item[De-identification] Removing or masking information that identifies a person.
  \item[Generative AI] AI that produces new content such as text, images, code, audio, or video.
  \item[Meaningful human review] Oversight by a qualified person with the authority, competence, and opportunity to understand, question, validate, modify, or override AI-assisted recommendations before consequential decisions are finalized (\cmo{}, Art.~II).
  \item[Personal data] Information that identifies or can reasonably identify an individual; see the Data Privacy Act.
  \item[Process evidence] Drafts, version history, notes, or reflections showing how work was produced.
  \item[Tier A/B/C] The three levels of tool-use review (open, listed, reviewed) in Section~\ref{sec:tools}.
  \item[Working days] Days excluding holidays, special non-working days, and days when work is suspended.
\end{description}
