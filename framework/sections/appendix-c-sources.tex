\section{Sources Consulted}\label{app:verification}

Statements of national policy in this draft were checked against the issued texts of \cmo{} \citep{cmo21} and \rmo{} \citep{rmo318}. Literature citations were checked against the full texts of the works listed in the References, and claims attributed to them are limited to what those works report. Where a source could not be consulted, no claim is attributed to it. Matters of VSU practice (roles, review targets, approval route, and AI-use levels) are proposals of the Technical Working Group for AI Policy and Governance and are submitted to the panel for review.
