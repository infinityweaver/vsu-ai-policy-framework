\section{Tool Approval and Procurement}\label{sec:tools}

A simple three-tier pathway keeps low-risk adoption frictionless and reserves scrutiny for high-risk uses.

\subsection{Principles for the tool list}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Maintain the list as a living document, updated by the Support Desk; do not duplicate reviews across colleges.
  \item Prefer tools with clear data-protection terms, no training on University inputs, export options, and accessibility features.
  \item Record vendor, version, data location, retention, and review date for Tier~B and C tools.
  \item Procurement follows applicable government procurement law and University procurement rules.
  \item Prohibited: tools with unacceptable security or privacy practices, and uses that are unlawful, discriminatory, or deceptive (e.g., covert emotion recognition or biometric surveillance without lawful basis and Tier~C approval).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Exceptions and pilots}
Faculty and units may run time-limited pilots of new tools for Tier~B use with a short notice to the Support Desk, to be reviewed afterwards. Pilots are encouraged and are intended to feed the list.
