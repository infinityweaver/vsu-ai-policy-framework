\subsection{Amendment procedure}\label{sec:amendment}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Proposal:} any member of the University community may propose an amendment through the Support Desk.
  \item \textbf{Screening:} the Steering Committee classifies the proposal as minor (clarification, tool list, template) or major (principles, tiers, integrity or due-process provisions, roles).
  \item \textbf{Minor changes:} approved by the Steering Committee, logged, and published.
  \item \textbf{Major changes:} brief consultation with faculty, students, and staff, then endorsement by the Steering Committee and approval by the same authority that approved the framework. \todoverify{Identify approving authority.}
  \item \textbf{Publication:} each version is dated, numbered, and accompanied by a change summary; the previous version is archived.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Transitional provisions}
For the first term after approval, course declarations are encouraged but not penalized if late; cases arising before a declaration was available are handled educationally.
