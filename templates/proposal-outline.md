## 1. Executive summary

Summarize the need for a university AI policy framework, its proposed scope, intended outcomes, and the decisions requested.

## 2. Context and rationale

Describe the institutional context, opportunities, risks, existing guidance, and relevant authoritative sources. Distinguish sourced facts from proposals.

## 3. Objectives and principles

List proposed objectives and principles for consideration. Explain how each would guide institutional choices.

## 4. Scope and definitions

Identify proposed covered communities, activities, systems, and exceptions. Define terms clearly; flag definitions requiring university determination.

## 5. Proposed framework areas

For each area, describe the issue, proposed direction, responsible roles (to be confirmed), implementation considerations, and unresolved questions. Potential areas may include teaching and learning, research, administration, data protection, accessibility, integrity, procurement, and risk management; inclusion here does not imply adoption.

## 6. Governance and implementation options

Describe possible oversight, responsibilities, support, training, reporting, monitoring, and review mechanisms for authorized consideration.

## 7. Consultation and deliberation plan

List proposed stakeholder groups, panels or bodies to consult, method, schedule, accessibility measures, and how comments will be recorded and dispositioned. Verify required university procedures.

## 8. Resource and impact considerations

Describe anticipated staffing, training, technology, financial, legal, equity, and operational considerations, with assumptions and evidence.
