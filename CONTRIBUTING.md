# Contributing

Thank you for helping develop the VSU AI Policy Framework. Contributions should support a transparent, careful review process and remain subject to the university's official governance and records procedures.

## Before contributing

- Read the [development process](docs/development-process.md) and check the current status of the draft or review stage.
- Confirm that material is approved for publication in this public repository. Do not upload confidential panel records, personal or sensitive information, or unpublished material restricted by university rules.
- When proposing text, state the section, rationale, and relevant evidence or references. Separate a proposed change from an adopted decision.

## Changes and review

- Use a pull request for substantive document changes. Keep each pull request focused and identify the draft version or review stage it concerns.
- Invite feedback from the appropriate reviewers; use neutral, respectful language and represent comments accurately.
- Address feedback by recording a disposition (accepted, partly accepted, not accepted, or referred for decision) and a brief rationale in the relevant review record.
- Do not mark proposals as approved or speak on behalf of a panel or university body unless formally authorized.
- Preserve the decision log and update process status when a stage is completed.

## Feedback that cannot be public

Do not put restricted or confidential comments in public issues, discussions, pull requests, or files. Submit them through the university-approved channel and, if appropriate, record only a non-sensitive summary and reference here after authorization.
