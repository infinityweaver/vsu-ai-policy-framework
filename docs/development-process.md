# Development process

This is a working lifecycle for documenting the framework's development. Confirm the responsible bodies, required procedures, and sequence with VSU; this guide does not replace university rules or establish authority.

## Suggested stages

| Stage | Record to maintain | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Planning | Scope, objectives, intended users, governance, schedule, and consultation plan | Proposal to draft is ready for authorized consideration |
| 2. Proposal | Problem statement, rationale, proposed principles and coverage, references, and open questions | Proposal version and submission date are recorded |
| 3. Initial drafting | Versioned framework draft and supporting materials | Draft is ready for the designated review process |
| 4. Panel review | One feedback record per panel or review round; preserve the draft version reviewed | Comments are dispositioned or explicitly left open |
| 5. Deliberation and revision | Meeting outcomes, decision log, revised drafts, and unresolved matters | Authorized reviewers complete the required deliberations |
| 6. Endorsement and approval | Endorsement or approval record and final text | Formal approval is evidenced by the competent university authority |
| 7. Publication and maintenance | Approved version, effective date if applicable, publication reference, and review owner | Official version and future maintenance route are clear |

## Review-round checklist

1. Identify the panel or review body, review round, dates, facilitator or recorder, and the exact draft version shared.
2. Collect comments through authorized channels. Obtain any necessary permission before placing comments or records in this public repository.
3. Consolidate feedback without changing its meaning. Use the [panel feedback template](../templates/panel-feedback.md).
4. Record a disposition and rationale for each comment. Escalate unresolved or policy-level choices to the appropriate authority rather than resolving them informally.
5. Revise the draft with version history and a summary of material changes.
6. Record deliberation outcomes and decisions in the [decision log](decision-log.md), including the responsible body and status.
7. Publish only material cleared for public release. Keep restricted records in approved university systems and link to them only when permitted.

## Status labels

Use clear, non-authoritative labels such as **Planning**, **Drafting**, **Under review**, **Revision in progress**, **For endorsement**, **For approval**, **Approved** (only with formal evidence), and **Superseded**. State who confirmed a status and when. A repository label or merge alone is not university approval.

## Final approval record

When formal approval occurs, record the approving authority, approval date, resolution or official reference, approved version identifier, and effective date if specified. Include links or copies only when public release is authorized. Do not infer approval from comments, consensus in a working group, or repository activity.
