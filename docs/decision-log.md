## Guidance

- Label proposals and recommendations as such; do not describe them as decisions until the competent body has decided.
- Record unresolved questions as **Open** and identify the authority or process needed to resolve them.
- For a formal approval, record the approving authority, date, approved version, and official reference. Do not mark the framework **Approved** without formal evidence.
- Protect confidential records and personal information; include only public, authorized references.
